About the Certificate & Purpose

This weekend trains students in consulting and intrapreneurship fundamentals problem discovery, rapid ideation, customer definition, and pitching an MVP. Teams will receive on-the-spot coaching and a certificate of participation.

Judging Criteria & Time

Judges will evaluate on the following main axes:

  • Problem — How well-defined & compelling is the problem?
  • Client — Who is the client, is the solution realistic?
  • MVP — Is there a clear minimum viable product?
  • Financially Successful — Plausibility of business/impact model.
  • Why Chosen — Clear rationale for selection of this idea over others.
  • Next Steps & Customer Outreach — Are next steps actionable & how will they reach customers?

Time limit for each pitch will be set by the client panel on Sunday — typically 3–5 minutes plus Q&A. Please practice with mentors throughout Saturday.
